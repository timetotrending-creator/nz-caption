Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free plan?

Yes! The free plan is forever-free, no credit card needed. You get auto-subtitles in 20+ languages, 10 templates, and MP4 export. Most casual creators don't need to upgrade.

What's in the 3-day trial?

The trial unlocks every Pro feature — 50+ templates, AI template builder, auto emoji, B-roll suggestions, translation, and more. No credit card required. After 3 days you can subscribe or continue on Free.

How does billing work?

Billing isn't enabled yet — we're in beta. When it launches, you'll be able to pay via Stripe (international) or JazzCash/EasyPaisa for Pakistan-based creators. Cancel anytime.

What languages does the auto-subtitle support?

English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Indonesian, Bengali, Tamil, Vietnamese, and more — 20+ total.

Will my videos be uploaded anywhere?

No. NZ Caption processes everything in your browser. Your videos never leave your device, which means it's fast, private, and you can edit even confidential content safely.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel any time from your dashboard. You'll keep premium features until the end of your billing period, then move back to the Free plan.
